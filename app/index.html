Black Friday, cross-examined.

We checked every price in this ledger at the store selling it, recorded the usual price beside the sale price, and looked for the same product at rival stores. Then we graded the discount — not the headline. Some of this week's loudest percentages do not survive the evidence.

How we grade: Genuine Deal — deep cut, corroborated. Good Price — fair, honestly framed. Questionable Discount — the percentage leans on a reference price shoppers rarely pay. Poor Value — a token cut; wait. Confidence reflects how much evidence backs the entry, and is graded separately.

No entries match that combination. Loosen a filter — the ledger is honest, but it is not infinite.

Methodology, in brief

Each entry was checked by a researcher during Black Friday week 2026. The sale price and usual price are the figures the selling store showed at check time; the check date and source ride with every entry. Cross-store prices are observations from the same checks, not live feeds. Where a store's “usual” price is a list price shoppers rarely pay, we say so — and grade the deal down for it. Prices move during Black Friday week; a price in this ledger is a checked fact about a moment, not a promise about tomorrow.

The Price Ledger is an independent publication. It takes no commission from the stores it investigates. Store links open the product page our researchers checked.
